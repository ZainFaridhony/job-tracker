# Project Brief: Job Tracker AI

## 1. Vision & Core Value Proposition
**Job Tracker AI** is a premium, intelligence-first career command center designed to transform the job search from a passive activity into a proactive, AI-driven journey. Inspired by the high-fidelity aesthetics of **Linear, Vercel, and Apple**, the platform serves as a "personal AI career assistant" that doesn't just track applications but actively optimizes them for success.

## 2. Design Language & Brand Identity
The platform follows a **"Premium Minimalist"** aesthetic, prioritizing clarity, white space, and high-quality micro-interactions.

*   **Design System Name:** Job Tracker AI Premium
*   **Color Palette:** 
    *   Primary Surface: `#FAFAFA` (Soft background)
    *   Containers: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with 24px corner radius.
    *   Accents: Deep Black (`#111111`) for primary actions and active states.
*   **Typography:** **Geist** (for that precision engineering feel) and Inter.
*   **Shadows:** Soft, deep elevations (`0 12px 40px rgba(0,0,0,.06)`).
*   **Logo:** A geometric, abstract "R" mark that signifies trajectory and growth.

## 3. Core Product Flows & Features

### A. Authentication & Recovery
A seamless entry experience that establishes trust through simplicity.
*   **Premium Sign-in/Sign-up:** Split-screen layout featuring a high-fidelity "94% Match" hero preview on the left and a clean, centered form on the right.
*   **Secure Recovery:** Minimalist "Forgot Password" and "Check Email" flows that maintain visual continuity.

### B. High-Fidelity Onboarding
A 6-step guided wizard that gathers the "data fuel" for the AI engine.
1.  **Resume Upload:** The foundation of the user profile.
2.  **Career Goals:** Defining targets and ambitions.
3.  **Job Preferences:** Location, remote status, and role types.
4.  **Skills & Experience:** Deep dive into technical and soft skills.
5.  **Work Preferences:** Salary targets and currency selection.
6.  **AI Personalization:** Final engine calibration.

### C. The Command Center (Post-Auth)
*   **Dashboard:** A proactive AI coach view providing a "Daily Briefing," application health scores, and upcoming interview preparation.
*   **Jobs Board:** A modern job discovery engine with AI-match scores, role cards, and a detailed "Role Drawer" for quick evaluation.
*   **Applications (Kanban):** A Linear-inspired workspace for managing the hiring funnel. 
    *   **Stages:** Wishlist, Applied, Interview, Offer, Rejected.
    *   **Detail Drawer:** Features side-by-side **Resume Comparison** (Current vs. Optimized), AI Portfolio Recommendations, and Interview Checklists.

## 4. Technical Design Principles
*   **One Platform Feeling:** Identical top navigation (centered pill design) across all views.
*   **Intelligence-First:** AI insights are integrated into the UI (match scores, improvement suggestions) rather than being hidden in a menu.
*   **Responsive:** Desktop-first precision with a fluid, container-based layout.
