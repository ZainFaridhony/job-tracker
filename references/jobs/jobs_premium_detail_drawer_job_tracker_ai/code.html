The Role

As a Senior Product Designer at Acme Corp, you will lead the design strategy for our core platform. You'll work closely with product managers and engineers to translate complex workflows into elegant, user-centric solutions. We're looking for someone who excels in high-growth environments.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead end-to-end design processes from discovery to high-fidelity handoff.
  • Maintain and evolve our design system to ensure consistency across products.
  • Conduct user research and usability testing to validate design decisions.
  • Mentor junior designers and contribute to a healthy design culture.
